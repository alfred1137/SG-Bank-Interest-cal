\## Singapore savings rates: 31 July 2026



\### DBS Multiplier Account



\*\*Bonus balance cap\*\*



\* Income + 1 category: first \*\*S$50,000\*\*

\* Income + 2 or more categories: first \*\*S$100,000\*\*

\* Excess: \*\*0.05% p.a.\*\*

\* Minimum total eligible transactions: \*\*S$500/month\*\* \[\\\[dbs.com.sg\\]](https://www.dbs.com.sg/personal/rates-online/multiplier-account.page), \[\\\[dbs.com.sg\\]](https://www.dbs.com.sg/personal/deposits/bank-earn/multiplier), \[\\\[sgfi.app\\]](https://sgfi.app/savings/dbs-multiplier)



| Monthly eligible transactions | Income + 1 category | Income + 2 | Income + 3+ |

| ----------------------------: | ------------------: | ---------: | ----------: |

|           S$500 to \\<S$15,000 |               1.80% |      2.10% |       2.40% |

|        S$15,000 to \\<S$30,000 |               1.90% |      2.20% |       2.50% |

|                     ≥S$30,000 |               2.20% |      3.00% |   \*\*4.10%\*\* |



\*\*Conditions\*\*



\* Income: salary, dividends, CPF or SRS payouts into DBS/POSB.

\* Other categories: credit card/PayLah! spending, home loan, insurance, investments.

\* Credit card and PayLah! spending count as \*\*one category\*\*.

\* Age ≤29: card/PayLah! spending without income gives \*\*1.50% on first S$50,000\*\*. \[\\\[dbs.com.sg\\]](https://www.dbs.com.sg/personal/deposits/bank-earn/multiplier), \[\\\[sgfi.app\\]](https://sgfi.app/savings/dbs-multiplier)



\*\*Last identifiable system revision:\*\* \*\*1 August 2023\*\*. No later official rate-revision notice found; official current maximum remains 4.10%. \[\\\[dbs.com.sg\\]](https://www.dbs.com.sg/personal/rates-online/multiplier-account.page), \[\\\[growbeansprout.com\\]](https://growbeansprout.com/dbs-multiplier-account-review)

\## Quick choice



\* \*\*S$150,000 + salary + normal card spend:\*\* UOB One, \*\*1.90% effective\*\*.

\* \*\*S$100,000 + salary + S$1,000 card spend:\*\* SC Bonus$aver, \*\*1.85%\*\*.

\* \*\*Existing DBS home loan/investment/insurance:\*\* DBS Multiplier; \*\*1.80% to 4.10%\*\*.

\* \*\*No conditions:\*\* CIMB FastSaver; blended \*\*1.053% on S$75,000\*\*.

\* \*\*Ignore headline alone:\*\* UOB 3.40% is top-tier, not whole-balance rate; SC 5.85% requires investment plus insurance.
